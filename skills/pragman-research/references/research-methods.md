# Research methods and evidence contract

## Selection

| Method | Use when | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quick reconnaissance | vocabulary or current landscape is unclear | the predeclared minimum evidence or cap is reached and a stop-or-deepen decision is explicit |
| Technical source-first | behavior depends on software, standards, APIs, or research | primary sources directly answer the implementation question |
| Deep multi-source | consequence is high or claims are contested | material claims are triangulated or disagreements are explicit |
| Independent fan-out | two or more questions have no dependency | every lane returns the same evidence schema and reconciliation is complete |
| Decision research | realistic alternatives compete | criteria, trade-offs, recommendation, and observable revisit-or-switch conditions are explicit |
| Internal workspace | private organizational context changes the answer | approved source aliases are covered without crossing context boundaries |

## Evidence record

For each material claim capture a stable source reference, source type, publication/event date, retrieval date, the claim it supports, whether support is direct or inferred, confidence, and limitations. In the delivered synthesis, present direct support separately from interpretation. Keep excerpts minimal and respect source-use limits.

## Source priority

Prefer original documentation, standards, laws, datasets, code, papers, company disclosures, and direct records. Use reputable analysis to add context or competing interpretation. Community reports can identify real failure patterns but do not by themselves establish prevalence.

## Synthesis

Lead with the recommended action. Separate facts from inference, explain source disagreements, identify what would change the recommendation, and state the lowest-cost next test. A source inventory without a decision synthesis is incomplete.
